Thursday, Oct 29: Ancient Sensō-ji, Chef Knives & Kuramae
📋 Day Preparation Notes
Today is a beautifully smooth, zero-transit walking day. Walk right out of your hotel to see Sensō-ji at its most silent and magical hour before the city wakes up.
07:30 Silent Morning @ Sensō-ji Temple & Kaminarimon
Step-by-Step Route & Interesting Facts
Tokyo's oldest temple (founded in 645 AD by two fisherman brothers who scooped a golden statue of Kannon from the Sumida River).
  1. STEP 1 Kaminarimon (Thunder Gate): Pass under the 700kg red paper lantern in perfect morning silence.
  2. STEP 2 Nakamise-dori: Walk down the 250-meter stone approach line before the shops open.
  3. STEP 3 Jokoro Incense Burner: Bathe your head and hands in sacred smoke for health.
  4. STEP 4 Main Hall (Hondo): Pay respects and draw an Omikuji fortune scroll.
08:45 Morning Coffee @ February Cafe
Breakfast Notes
Famous for thick-cut, buttered Shokupan (Japanese milk bread) toast and excellent pour-over coffee.
