DAY 1: ARRIVAL, RAMEN & EBISU NIGHTS

Wed, Oct 14
🎯 Day Preparation Notes Welcome to Tokyo! Today is about smooth transit, checking into Ebisu, and a relaxed night of local ramen, Kochi-style crispy gyozas, and craft cocktails.

14:00 Landing @ Narita Airport (NRT)

Flight CA929 lands at Terminal 1 at 14:00.

💡 Arrival Logistics & Transit
  1. STEP 1 Clear passport control and collect luggage.
  2. STEP 2 Add digital Suica cards to Apple Wallet (or buy physical Welcome Suica at ticket counter).
  3. STEP 3 Get cash from a 7-Eleven ATM in the terminal.
🚇 Express Transit to Ebisu (~65 mins): Take the Keisei Skyliner train from Narita T1 direct to Nippori Station (36 mins), then transfer to the JR Yamanote Line heading south to Ebisu Station (30 mins).

17:00 Check-in @ STARRY STAY RESIDENCE EBISU

Unpack and settle into your West Tokyo home base. Ebisu is a sophisticated, residential hub favored by Tokyo locals for high-end craft izakayas, third-wave coffee, and quiet side streets.

19:00 Comfort Ramen @ Chorori Ebisu

A classic, authentic local neighborhood ramen shop. Famous for their rich Shoyu Ramen topped with charred scallion oil and their incredible house-made Chahan (Japanese fried rice).

20:30 Crispy Gyoza @ Ebisu Yasubei

Kochi-style thin-skin crispy gyozas pan-fried to perfection. The ultimate side snack with a cold local draft beer.

21:30 Craft Cocktails @ Bar TRIAD

Elegantly lit 3rd-floor botanical cocktail lounge in Ebisu. Perfect relaxed atmosphere to close out your arrival night.

🍽️ Day 1 Meal & Beverage Summary

  • Ramen Dinner: Chorori Ebisu — Shoyu scallion-oil ramen & fried rice.
  • Gyoza Stop: Ebisu no Yasube — Crispy pan-fried Kochi gyozas.
  • Cocktails: Bar TRIAD — Botanical craft drinks near Ebisu Station.

DAY 2: COFFEE, LOCAL FOOD & TEAMLAB

Thu, Oct 15
🎯 Day Preparation Notes CONFIRMED BOOKING: teamLab Borderless (2 Adults, ¥10,800 total). Admission window is 13:00 - 13:30. Arrive by 12:45. Wear light or white clothing so light projections reflect off your body!

08:30 Morning Coffee @ SARUTAHIKO COFFEE (Ebisu Main Shop)

Start your first full morning at this legendary Ebisu flagship roastery. Exceptional single-origin pour-overs and Japanese iced coffees right by Ebisu station.

09:30 Canelé & Specialty Coffee @ JOE TALK COFFEE

Stroll past JOE TALK COFFEE in Ebisu. Grab one of their signature freshly baked in-house canelés for a sweet morning bite.

10:30 Architecture & Books @ Daikanyama T-Site

Designed by Klein Dytham Architecture, this modern complex features three pavilion buildings connected by a canopy path. (Optional stop at Binya Coffee nearby for classic Japanese coffee & firm Purin caramel pudding).

11:30 100% Japanese Lunch @ Daikanyama (Pre-teamLab)

Enjoy a authentic local Japanese lunch set (Teishoku) before heading to teamLab:

  • Choice 1 (Teishoku): Rice Code / Tamashii no Shuukaku (Daikanyama) — Traditional Japanese set meal with freshly cooked iron-pot rice, grilled fish/wagyu, and seasonal side dishes.
  • Choice 2 (Soba): Daikanyama Matsuen — Handmade Japanese buckwheat soba noodles.
🚇 Transit to teamLab (~15 mins): Take the Metro directly from Ebisu/Daikanyama to Kamiyacho Station (Exit 5) for Azabudai Hills.

13:00 Digital Art @ teamLab Borderless [CONFIRMED TICKET]

Admission Window: 13:00 - 13:30. MORI Building Digital Art Museum integrated into Thomas Heatherwick's undulating Azabudai Hills plaza.

🚇 Transit to Shibuya (~15 mins): Take the Metro back to Ebisu, then switch to the JR Yamanote Line up one stop to Shibuya.

17:00 Shibuya Sunset & Shopping

See Shibuya Scramble Crossing as the evening lights turn on. Check out Uniqlo / GU Shibuya for any light clothing needs before returning to Ebisu.

20:00 Japanese Street Food Crawl @ Ebisu Yokocho

Head back to Ebisu (3 mins by train) to eat through the historic retro indoor alley packed with authentic mini-stalls:

  • Niku Zushi (肉寿司): Famous Wagyu beef sushi, seared with a blowtorch over sushi rice.
  • Uomaru (魚○): Fresh Sea of Japan seafood & fish grilled over charcoal (robatayaki).
  • Nikusaku (肉さく): Classic charcoal-grilled yakitori chicken & bacon-wrapped vegetable skewers.

🍽️ Day 2 Meal & Beverage Summary

  • Morning Coffee: Sarutahiko Coffee Ebisu Main Shop & JOE TALK COFFEE.
  • Japanese Lunch: Traditional Japanese Teishoku (set meal) or handmade Soba in Daikanyama.
  • Street Food Dinner: Ebisu Yokocho (Niku Zushi, Uomaru charcoal seafood, Nikusaku yakitori).

DAY 3: COASTAL EXCURSION

Fri, Oct 16
🎯 Day Preparation Notes (Early Departure) IMPORTANT: Early train departure at 07:30 AM! Pick up fresh egg salads (tamago sando) + canned coffee from Lawson/7-Eleven to eat before leaving.
🚆 Transit to Kamakura (~50 mins): Take the direct JR Shonan-Shinjuku Line train from Ebisu Station straight to Kamakura Station.

08:15 Bamboo Forest & Matcha @ Hokokuji Temple

Sip freshly whisked ceremonial matcha inside 2,000 towering bamboo stalks.

13:30 Great Buddha (Daibutsu) @ Kotoku-in

The iconic 13.35-meter open-air bronze Great Buddha statue, cast in 1252.

14:30 Coastal Views @ Hase-dera Temple

Enjoy sweeping views across Sagami Bay while having green tea ice cream.

16:30 Enoshima Island & Sea Candle Sunset 🌇

Walk across the pedestrian bridge to Enoshima Island for sunset views over Sagami Bay.

🍽️ Day 3 Meal & Beverage Plan

  • Lunch (12:00): Matsubara-an (Kamakura). Incredible handmade soba institution housed inside a restored early-20th-century Japanese coastal villa.
  • Dinner (18:30): Tobiccho (Enoshima). Eat fresh or boiled baby sardine rice bowls (Shirasu-don).

DAY 4: VINTAGE, CATS & SHINJUKU

Sat, Oct 17
🎯 Day Preparation Notes Move smoothly from morning shrines to vintage shopping, a hidden cat temple, a retro neighborhood tram, and sunset in Shinjuku.

08:00 Shinto Morning Ritual @ Meiji Jingu

Arrive early to observe the 08:00 AM morning Shinto ritual (Nikku-sai) conducted by priests in the main courtyard.

13:00 Vinyl & Vintage @ Shimokitazawa

Dig through record stores and endless vintage clothing racks in Tokyo's bohemian neighborhood.

15:15 The Cat Temple @ Gotokuji

Quiet temple packed with thousands of white beckoning cat statues left for good luck.

16:30 Golden Hour Sunset 🌇 @ Metro Building

45th-floor observatory for free golden hour views across Tokyo.

17:45 Yakitori & Alleyways @ Omoide Yokocho

Preserved 1940s post-war alleyway network ("Memory Lane") packed with smoky counter-only yakitori stalls.

🍽️ Day 4 Meal & Beverage Plan

  • Lunch (11:30): Maisen Aoyama. Famous tonkatsu housed inside a renovated Showa-era public bathhouse.
  • Dinner (19:30): Ramen Nagi Golden Gai. Famous for intense broth made from 20 varieties of dried sardine.
