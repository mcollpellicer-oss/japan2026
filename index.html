10:00 Architecture & Books @ Daikanyama T-Site (Tsutaya)

Designed by Klein Dytham Architecture, this modern complex features three pavilion buildings connected by a canopy path. The white exterior wall tiles form an interlocking "T" pattern.

08:15 Bamboo Forest & Matcha @ Hokokuji Temple
Step-by-Step Route & Tea Ceremony
Take the direct JR Shonan-Shinjuku Line train from Ebisu Station (50 mins) straight to Kamakura Station. Hokokuji is Kamakura’s finest quiet bamboo temple.
  1. STEP 1 Main Gate: Enter past the moss garden towards the main Zen hall.

  3. STEP 3 Tatami Seating: Sip freshly whisked ceremonial matcha with a dry sugar confection (higashi).

13:30 Great Buddha (Daibutsu) @ Kotoku-in
Historical Facts & Context
The iconic 13.35-meter open-air bronze Great Buddha statue, cast in 1252. The massive wooden temple building that originally housed it was destroyed by a tsunami in 1498, leaving the bronze statue standing under the open sky ever since.

Saturday, Oct 17: Harajuku Shinto, Architecture & Shinjuku Neon
📋 Day Preparation Notes
Arrive at Meiji Jingu early (08:00 AM) to experience the quiet morning Shinto rituals before weekend crowds arrive in Harajuku.
08:00 Shinto Morning Ritual @ Meiji Jingu Shrine
Step-by-Step Route & Interesting Facts
Dedicated to Emperor Meiji and Empress Shoken. The 170-acre forest was artificially planted in 1920 with 120,000 trees donated from across Japan, designed to become a self-sustaining ecosystem.
  1. STEP 1 Harajuku Torii Gate: Bow slightly before entering under the 12-meter tall wooden Torii gate.
  2. STEP 2 Sake Barrel Wall: Walk past decorative barrels of sake (meishoshu) donated by breweries across Japan.
  3. STEP 3 Temizuya Water Basin: Purify your left hand, right hand, and mouth before entering the main courtyard.
  4. STEP 4 Main Courtyard: Observe the 08:00 AM morning Shinto ritual (Nikku-sai) conducted by priests in white robes.

Wednesday, Oct 28: Return to East Tokyo & Riverside Landing
📋 Day Preparation Notes
Welcome back to Tokyo from your regional trip! Check into your East Tokyo (Asakusa/Kuramae) hotel, settle in, and get ready for a relaxed shitamachi evening along the Sumida River.
15:30 SANAA Architecture @ Sumida Hokusai Museum
Architecture & Art Context
Striking angular mirrored-aluminum building designed by Pritzker Prize winner Kazuyo Sejima (SANAA). Houses the works of famous woodblock artist Katsushika Hokusai, creator of The Great Wave off Kanagawa.
17:30 Historic "Electric Brandy" @ Kamiya Bar (1880)
Historical Drinks & Standing Bar Notes
Japan's first Western-style bar, operating out of an official 1921 Taisho-era building. Order a standing glass of Denki Bran ("Electric Brandy"), a Meiji-era herbal spirit blend.
19:00 Izakaya Dinner & Stew @ Hoppy Street
Food Notes & All Backup Options
A 1950s post-war drinking street lined with plastic-curtain izakayas. Famous for slow-simmered beef tendon stew (nikomi) and low-alcohol Hoppy mixed with shochu.
  • BACKUP 1 Yakiniku Beast: High-grade Wagyu beef yakiniku izakaya.
  • BACKUP 2 Asakusa Ichimon: Historic Edo-style tuna hotpot (negi-ma nabe) inside a traditional merchant house.

07:30 Early Morning @ Sensō-ji Temple & Kaminarimon
Step-by-Step Route & Interesting Facts
Tokyo's oldest temple (founded in 645 AD by two fisherman brothers who scooped a golden statue of Kannon from the Sumida River).
  1. STEP 1 Kaminarimon (Thunder Gate): Pass under the 700kg red paper lantern.
  2. STEP 2 Nakamise-dori: Walk down the quiet 250-meter stone approach.
  3. STEP 3 Jokoro Incense Burner: Bathe your head and hands in sacred smoke for health.
  4. STEP 4 Main Hall (Hondo): Pay respects and draw an Omikuji fortune scroll.
  • Breakfast (08:45): Thick-cut Shokupan milk-bread toast & pour-over coffee at February Cafe.
