DAY 1: TOKYO ARRIVAL

Tuesday, Oct 20
🎯 DAY 1 TOP TIPS & STRATEGY
  • Pasmo / Suica Card: Load your digital Suica or Pasmo card into Apple Wallet immediately upon landing for seamless subway tapping.
  • Jet-Lag Navigation: Keep night 1 relaxed with an unhurried walk through neon-lit alleyways rather than rigid reservations.

03:00 PM Airport Arrival & Base Check-In

Land at Tokyo Narita/Haneda. Board the Narita Express (N'EX) or Tokyo Monorail directly into the city. Check into your Shibuya/Shinjuku base, unpack, and freshen up.

06:00 PM Neon Alleyways & Alley Exploration

Kick off the trip with an evening stroll through Shibuya Crossing and the quiet side-streets of Oku-Shibuya, or explore the lantern-lit wooden stalls of Omoide Yokocho and Golden Gai in Shinjuku.

💡 Cultural Context: Omoide Yokocho & Golden Gai

Omoide Yokocho ("Memory Lane"): A dense network of narrow alleyways dating back to the post-WWII 1940s, famous for smoky counter-seating yakitori stalls cooked over charcoal.

Golden Gai: A architectural time-capsule of six narrow passages packed with over 200 tiny, microscopic bars, preserved amid Shinjuku's modern skyscraper rise.

📍 Open Day 1 Evening Walk Map

🍽️ Day 1 Meal & Beverage Plan

  • Dinner: Counter yakitori at Toriyoshi Shibuya or smoky grilled skewers in Omoide Yokocho.
  • Drinks / Nightcap: Craft cocktails at The SG Club (ranked among Asia's best) or vinyl listening bar Bar Music in Shibuya.

DAY 2: TRADITION & IMMERSIVE DIGITAL ART

Wednesday, Oct 21
🎯 DAY 2 TOP TIPS & STRATEGY
  • 07:30 AM Senso-ji Start: Asakusa's Senso-ji is open 24/7. Visiting at 7:30 AM allows you to enjoy the temple grounds in absolute silence before Nakamise-dori crowds arrive.
  • teamLab Booking: Book your teamLab Planets timed slot for 01:30 PM well in advance. Wear pants that roll up above the knees (water exhibits are knee-deep).

07:30 AM Asakusa & Senso-ji Temple

Walk through the iconic Kaminarimon Gate into Senso-ji before the crowds. Explore the serene side-courtyards and 5-story pagoda.

💡 Cultural Context: Senso-ji Temple

Senso-ji: Tokyo's oldest temple, founded in 645 AD after two fisherman brothers pulled a golden statue of Kannon (Goddess of Mercy) from the Sumida River. The giant red paper lantern at Kaminarimon gate weighs over 700 kg.

10:30 AM Tsukiji Outer Market

Head to Tsukiji Outer Market for late-morning seafood bites, tamagoyaki (rolled omelet), and fresh oysters.

01:30 PM teamLab Planets Toyosu

Immerse yourselves in teamLab Planets—a barefoot sensory art museum where you wade through water filled with digital koi fish and walk through infinite mirror gardens.

📍 Open Day 2 Route Map

🍽️ Day 2 Meal & Beverage Plan

  • Breakfast / Morning Stop: Matcha latte at Hatoya Asakusa or fresh melonpan bakery near Senso-ji.
  • Lunch (Tsukiji): Fresh kaisen-don (seafood bowl) at Tsukiji Kagura or tamagoyaki skewers at Yamacho.
  • Coffee Break: Fuglen Tokyo or Kissa Nichinichi.
  • Dinner (Ginza): High-grade sushi set at Ginza Onodera or refined tonkatsu at Ginza Bairin.

DAY 3: FOREST SHRINE & DESIGN DISTRICTS

Thursday, Oct 22
🎯 DAY 3 TOP TIPS & STRATEGY
  • Meiji Jingu Sanctuary: Enter from the Harajuku side through the massive cedar Torii gate into the 170-acre forest.
  • Shinkansen Prep: Pack light for tomorrow morning's Shinkansen ride to Kanazawa.

08:30 AM Meiji Jingu Shrine Forest

Walk through the peaceful 170-acre evergreen forest surrounding Meiji Jingu. View the massive sake barrel wall (*Kazari-daru*) offered to the spirits of Emperor Meiji.

11:00 AM Omotesando & Cat Street Architectural Stroll

Wander the tree-lined boulevard of Omotesando, inspecting flagship architecture (Prada building by Herzog & de Meuron) and quiet coffee shops along Cat Street.

04:30 PM Shibuya Sky Sunset

Ascend to Shibuya Sky rooftop observation deck for 360-degree sunset views over Mount Fuji and the Tokyo skyline.

📍 Open Day 3 Route Map

🍽️ Day 3 Meal & Beverage Plan

  • Coffee Break: Chacha Koubou or Shozo Coffee Store in Omotesando.
  • Lunch: Soba noodles at Chikuyotei or artisan burgers at The Great Burger.
  • Dinner: Modern izakaya at Den Kushi Zaiuchi or supreme wagyu at Yoroniku.

DAY 15: TOKYO RETURN & GRAND FINALE

Wednesday, Oct 28
🎯 DAY 15 TOP TIPS & STRATEGY
  • Mountain-to-City Transition: Descend from Koyasan morning fire ceremony, collect bags in Osaka Namba, and board the Tokaido Shinkansen straight back to Tokyo Station (~2.5h).

02:30 PM Tokyo Base Arrival

Arrive via Bullet Train at Tokyo Station. Check into your Phase 5 finale hotel in Ginza / Nihonbashi.

05:30 PM Yurakucho Brick Arches & Ginza Stroll

Explore the vibrant lantern-lit yakitori stalls under the brick train arches in Yurakucho Gado-shita, followed by a evening walk along Ginza's illuminated shopping avenues.

🍽️ Day 15 Meal & Beverage Plan

  • Dinner: Smoky yakitori under the train tracks at Yurakucho Gado-shita.
  • Drinks: Classic Ginza cocktail experience at Bar High Five or Bar Tender.

DAY 16: OLD TOKYO & NAKAMEGURO CANAL

Thursday, Oct 29

09:00 AM Yanaka & Nezu Historic Neighborhoods

Stroll through Yanaka Ginza, one of Tokyo's few surviving pre-war neighborhoods. Visit the red torii tunnel at Nezu Shrine.

02:00 PM Daikanyama & Nakameguro Canal

Explore the stunning Daikanyama T-Site (Tsutaya Books) complex, then walk down along the peaceful Meguro River canal in Nakameguro.

📍 Open Day 16 Route Map

🍽️ Day 16 Meal & Beverage Plan

  • Coffee: Onibus Coffee Nakameguro (drinking single-origin drip right by the train tracks).
  • Lunch: Craft pizza at Pizzeria e Trattoria da Isa or artisan ramen at AFURI Nakameguro.
  • Dinner: Modern Kappo dinner at Aoyama Citron or cozy izakaya in Ebisu.

DAY 17: FAREWELL TOKYO

Friday, Oct 30

09:30 AM Kappabashi Kitchen Town (Craft Souvenirs)

Browse Kappabashi Street for handcrafted Japanese kitchen knives, lacquerware, ceramics, and handmade tea sets.

02:00 PM Imperial Palace East Gardens

Take a quiet afternoon stroll through the stone ramparts and manicured lawns of the Imperial Palace East Gardens.

🍽️ Day 17 Farewell Meal Plan

  • Farewell Dinner: Premium Omakase Sushi experience at Sushi Tokami or Sushi Yoshitake.
  • Final Toast: Rooftop drinks at Andaz Tokyo Rooftop Bar overlooking Tokyo Tower.
