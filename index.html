Oct 14: Arrival & Ebisu Landing

Cultural Notes & Logistics
Flight CA929 lands at Terminal 1 at 14:00. Clear passport control, collect luggage, and add physical or digital Suica IC cards to Apple Wallet.

Take the Keisei Skyliner train from Narita Terminal 1 direct to Nippori Station (36 mins), then transfer to the JR Yamanote Line down to Ebisu Station (30 mins).

17:00 Check-in @ STARRY STAY RESIDENCE EBISU
Cultural Notes & Logistics
Unpack and settle into your West Tokyo home base. Ebisu is a sophisticated, low-key residential hub favored by Tokyo locals for high-end craft izakayas, specialty coffee, and quiet side streets.
🚶 Walk along Ebisu-dori 5 mins walk
On the way: Stroll down Ebisu-dori street, observing local standing tachinomi bars, neighborhood bakeries, and quiet lantern-lit side alleys.
🗺️ View Walking Map
19:00 Dinner @ Ebisu no Yasube
Cultural Notes & Logistics
Specializes in Kochi-style thin-skin crispy gyoza made fresh to order. The dumplings are pan-fried to a golden shatter-crisp texture.
  • What to order: Yaki-gyoza (pan-fried dumplings), boiled gyoza in soup, paired with cold Ebisu draft beer or regional sake.
  • BACKUP Option 1: Ebisu Yokocho — A lively indoor alley packed with 20 distinct street-food stalls serving yakitori and grilled seafood.
  • BACKUP Option 2: Afuri Ebisu — Famous for light, refreshing Yuzu Shio (citrus-salt) ramen topped with charcoal-grilled chashu pork.
🚶 Side Street Walk 4 mins walk
Head into Ebisu's quiet backstreets towards the secret coffee shop counter.
🗺️ View Walking Map
21:00 Speakeasy Nightcap @ Janai Coffee
Cultural Notes & Logistics
A hidden cocktail lounge disguised as a minimalist third-wave coffee shop counter.
  • Secret Entry Trick: Open their website (janaicoffee.tokyo) on your smartphone, trace the secret gesture pattern on the screen menu to reveal the secret passkey, and present it to the barista.
  • BACKUP Option: Bar Tram — Speakeasy cocktail lounge specializing in herbal absinthe drips and craft spirits.
Oct 15: Meguro Canal, teamLab & Listening Bars
08:30 Specialty Coffee @ ONIBUS Coffee Nakameguro
Cultural Notes & Logistics
One of Tokyo's pioneering third-wave roasteries. Housed in a tiny wooden building tucked directly beside the Tokyu Toyoko train tracks and a local park.
  • Highlights: Order a single-origin Ethiopian or Guatemalan pour-over coffee. Head up to the second-floor wooden loft to sip your coffee while watching trains pass right outside the window frames.
🚶 Stroll uphill along Meguro Canal & Daikanyama backstreets 8 mins walk
On the way: Walk along the quiet Meguro River canal, then head uphill through Daikanyama’s tree-lined residential side alleys, passing independent Japanese fashion boutiques, architectural houses, and design studios.
🗺️ View Walking Map
10:00 Architecture & Books @ Daikanyama T-Site (Tsutaya)
Cultural Notes & Logistics
Designed by Klein Dytham Architecture, this modern complex features three pavilion buildings connected by a canopy path. The white exterior wall tiles form a intricate interlocking "T" pattern.
  • What to explore: World-class collections of design books, vintage architecture journals, Japanese photography monographs, and rare vinyl records.
  • Lunch (11:30): Dine on the outdoor garden patio at IVY Place inside the T-Site woods.
🚇 Metro Transit to teamLab ~15 mins
Walk 6 minutes back to Ebisu Station. Board the Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line directly to Kamiyacho Station (Exit 5), which connects indoors to Azabudai Hills.
🗺️ View Transit Route
13:00 Digital Art @ teamLab Borderless (Azabudai Hills)
Cultural Notes & Logistics
Integrated into Thomas Heatherwick's undulating Azabudai Hills urban design plaza. Unlike Planets, Borderless features digital artworks that freely wander across room boundaries, interacting with each other and visitors.
  • Visitor Tip: Wear white or light-colored clothing so the light projections reflect clearly onto you. Reserved entry slot: 13:00.
  • Post-Art Stroll (15:30): Walk Thomas Heatherwick’s outdoor green roofs and plazas in Azabudai Hills.
🚇 Return to Ebisu Base ~10 mins
Take the Hibiya Metro Line back to Ebisu Station to freshen up before evening drinks.
🗺️ View Transit Route
18:30 Craft Cocktails @ Bar Trench
Cultural Notes & Logistics
Consistently ranked among Asia's 50 Best Bars. An intimate, dimly-lit cocktail den staffed by master mixologists serving herbal spirits, absinthe, and bespoke cocktails.
21:45 Audiophile Listening @ BAR MARTHA
Cultural Notes & Logistics
Tokyo’s premier high-fidelity vinyl audio sanctuary. Features handcrafted vintage Tannoy speakers, tube amplifiers, rare jazz/soul vinyl pressings, and single-malt whiskies.
  • Rules of Etiquette: Photography is strictly prohibited inside. Conversations must be kept to a low whisper to honor the acoustics of the sound system.
Oct 16: Weekday Coastal Excursion — Kamakura & Enoshima
08:15 Bamboo Forest @ Hokokuji Temple
Cultural Notes & Logistics
Take the direct JR Shonan-Shinjuku Line train from Ebisu Station (50 mins) straight to Kamakura Station. Hokokuji is Kamakura’s finest quiet bamboo temple.
  • Matcha Experience: Purchase a tea ticket at the gate to sit inside the open-air wooden tatami tea house, enjoying freshly whisked ceremonial matcha while looking out over 2,000 towering green bamboo stalks.
🚶 Stroll through Kamakura's historic streets 15 mins walk
On the way: Walk past Tsurugaoka Hachimangu Shrine, then stroll down Komachi-dori pedestrian street. Stop at Kamakura Beniya to buy their legendary Kurumicco caramel-walnut pastries packaged in retro squirrel tins.
🗺️ View Walking Map
12:00 Villa Soba Lunch @ Matsubara-an
Cultural Notes & Logistics
An incredible handmade soba institution housed inside a restored early-20th-century Japanese coastal villa surrounded by trees.
  • What to order: Freshly rolled buckwheat soba with crispy duck tempura or fresh local seafood tempura.
🚶 Walk through Hase residential neighborhood 12 mins walk
On the way: Stroll through Hase’s peaceful residential alleys, passing traditional wooden houses and small seaside bakeries.
🗺️ View Walking Map
13:30 Great Buddha (Daibutsu) @ Kotoku-in
Cultural Notes & Logistics
The iconic 13.35-meter open-air bronze Great Buddha statue, cast in 1252. The massive wooden temple building that originally housed it was destroyed by a tsunami in 1498, leaving the bronze statue standing under the open sky ever since.
14:30 Coastal Views @ Hase-dera Temple
Cultural Notes & Logistics
A hillside Zen temple famous for its gilded 11-headed wooden statue of Kannon and beautiful landscape gardens. Walk up to the upper observation terrace for panoramic views over Sagami Bay.
🚃 Board retro Enoden Coastal Streetcar 20 mins ride
On the way: Ride the retro green Enoden electric streetcar along the Pacific shoreline, passing the famous ocean crossing at Kamakura-kokomae.
🗺️ View Route Map
16:30 Enoshima Island & Sea Candle Sunset
Cultural Notes & Logistics
Walk across the pedestrian bridge to Enoshima Island. Walk up shrine steps to the Enoshima Sea Candle observation tower for sunset views over Sagami Bay (and Mt. Fuji on clear days).
  • Seafood Dinner (18:30): Eat fresh or boiled baby sardine rice bowls (Shirasu-don) and grilled turban shell at Tobiccho.
  • Nightcap (20:30): Train back to Ebisu; vinyl nightcap at A10 Bar (hidden behind fake coin lockers).
Oct 29: Sensō-ji, Chef Knives & Kuramae
07:30 Early Morning @ Sensō-ji Temple & Kaminarimon
Cultural Notes & Logistics
Tokyo's oldest temple (founded in 645 AD). Visiting at 7:30 AM allows you to walk through Kaminarimon Gate and down Nakamise-dori in complete morning silence before tourist crowds arrive.
Breakfast (08:45): Thick-cut Shokupan milk-bread toast & pour-over coffee at February Cafe.
🚶 Walk to Kappabashi Kitchenware Town 10 mins walk
On the way: Stroll through Asakusa’s old residential lanes heading west toward the kitchenware street.
🗺️ View Walking Map
09:30 Hand-Forged Knives & Ceramics @ Kappabashi
Cultural Notes & Logistics
The premier street in Tokyo for buying hand-forged Japanese chef knives and artisan family gifts.
  • Knife Shops: Visit Kama-Asa and Tsubaya for high-end hand-forged blades with complimentary custom English/Kanji name-engraving.
  • Ceramics & Craft: Visit Dengama for pottery, Tsuchi-ya for glassware, and Kanaya Brush for traditional boar-bristle brushes.
12:00 110-Year Buckwheat Soba @ Namiki Yabusoba
Cultural Notes & Logistics
Founded in 1913, this historic soba institution serves handmade buckwheat noodles with a concentrated, dark dipping broth.
  • BACKUP Option 1: Dote no Iseya — Historic tempura institution operating in a wooden building that survived the 1923 earthquake.
  • BACKUP Option 2: Unatoto — Casual charcoal-grilled eel over rice.
🚶 Stroll south into Kuramae ("Tokyo's Brooklyn") 12 mins walk
On the way: Walk along the river towards Kuramae’s renovated industrial warehouse district.
🗺️ View Walking Map
13:30 Custom Stationery & Craft Chocolate @ Kuramae
Cultural Notes & Logistics
Order custom hand-bound notebooks and bespoke bottled fountain pen ink at Kakimori. Enjoy single-origin bean-to-bar hot chocolate inside a renovated factory at Dandelion Chocolate.
  • Coffee (15:30): World-class light-roast pour-over coffee at Leaves Coffee Roasters or organic green tea at Nakamura Tea Life Store.
