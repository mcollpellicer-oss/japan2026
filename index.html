DAY 1: ARRIVAL & EBISU

Wed, Oct 14
🎯 Day Preparation Notes Welcome to Tokyo! Today is about smooth transit, securing IC cards, checking into your Ebisu base, and having an easy, high-quality arrival dinner without jetlag stress.

14:00 Landing @ Narita Airport (NRT)

Flight CA929 lands at Terminal 1 at 14:00.

💡 Arrival Logistics & Transit
  1. STEP 1 Clear passport control and collect luggage.
  2. STEP 2 Add digital Suica cards to Apple Wallet (or buy physical Welcome Suica at train ticket counter).
  3. STEP 3 Get cash from a 7-Eleven ATM in the terminal.
🚇 Express Transit to Ebisu (~65 mins): Take the Keisei Skyliner train from Narita T1 direct to Nippori Station (36 mins), then transfer to the JR Yamanote Line heading south to Ebisu Station (30 mins).

17:00 Check-in @ STARRY STAY RESIDENCE EBISU

Unpack and settle into your West Tokyo home base. Ebisu is a sophisticated, residential hub favored by Tokyo locals for high-end craft izakayas, third-wave coffee, and quiet, clean side streets without the chaos of Shinjuku.

🚶 Ebisu-dori Stroll: Stroll down Ebisu-dori street, taking in your first sights of local standing tachinomi bars, neighborhood bakeries, and glowing red lanterns in side alleys.

🍽️ Day 1 Meal & Beverage Plan

  • Dinner: Ebisu no Yasube (19:00). Kochi-style thin-skin crispy gyoza. Order Yaki-gyoza (pan-fried) and su-gyoza (boiled in soup) with cold draft beer.
  • BACKUP 1 Ebisu Yokocho: A lively indoor alley packed with 20 distinct street-food stalls serving yakitori and oden.
  • BACKUP 2 Afuri Ebisu: Famous for light Yuzu Shio (citrus-salt) ramen topped with charcoal-grilled chashu pork.
  • Nightcap: Janai Coffee (21:00). A hidden speakeasy disguised as a coffee shop. Secret Entry: Open janaicoffee.tokyo on your phone, trace the gesture pattern on the screen, and present the passkey to the barista.
  • BACKUP Bar Tram: Speakeasy cocktail lounge specializing in herbal absinthe drips.

DAY 2: DIGITAL ART & SHIBUYA

Thu, Oct 15
🎯 Day Preparation Notes Moderate walking day. Wear light or white clothing today for teamLab Borderless so the immersive light projections reflect brilliantly off your body. You will finish the evening under the neon lights of Shibuya!

08:30 Morning Coffee @ ONIBUS Nakameguro

One of Tokyo's pioneering third-wave roasteries housed in a tiny wooden building tucked directly beside the Tokyu Toyoko train tracks. Grab pastries from nearby I'm Donut? Nakameguro before sitting.

🚶 Meguro Canal Walk (12 mins): Walk along the quiet Meguro River canal, then head uphill through Daikanyama’s tree-lined residential side alleys.

10:00 Architecture & Books @ Daikanyama T-Site

Designed by Klein Dytham Architecture, this modern complex features three pavilion buildings connected by a canopy path.

🚇 Transit to teamLab (~15 mins): Walk 6 mins back to Ebisu Station. Board the Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line directly to Kamiyacho Station (Exit 5).

13:00 Digital Art @ teamLab Borderless

Integrated into Thomas Heatherwick's undulating Azabudai Hills urban plaza.

💡 Step-by-Step Exploration
  1. STEP 1 Borderless World: Wander the shifting waterfall rooms.
  2. STEP 2 Bubble Universe: Hundreds of glowing glass spheres reacting to your motion.
  3. STEP 3 EN TEA HOUSE: Digital flowers bloom inside your teacup.
🚇 Transit to Shibuya (~15 mins): Take the Hibiya Line back to Ebisu, then switch to the JR Yamanote Line up one stop to Shibuya.

17:15 Shibuya Scramble & Hachikō Statue

Arrive exactly as the sun sets and the massive digital billboards light up the crossing. Pay respects to the Hachikō Statue, then cross the main intersection diagonally.

18:00 Drinks & Neon Alleys @ Nonbei Yokocho

Contrast Shibuya's hyper-modern neon with its preserved post-war food alleys. Tucked beside the JR tracks, "Drunkard's Alley" features tiny 5-seat bars. Grab a quick yakitori skewer and a drink here!

🍽️ Day 2 Meal & Beverage Plan

  • Lunch (11:30): Garden patio dining at IVY Place inside T-Site.
  • BACKUP LUNCH Spring Valley Brewery: Craft beer tasting flights and wood-fired sourdough pizzas.
  • Afternoon Coffee (16:00): Kyoto-style Spanish lattes at % Arabica Azabudai Hills.
  • Dinner: Ebisu Yamaimo Sakai (19:30). Specializes in mountain yam dishes, teppanyaki, and seasonal izakaya plates.
  • BACKUP DINNER Toriyoshi Ebisu: High-end yakitori grilled over Kishu Binchotan charcoal.
  • Nightcap: Bar Trench (21:00). Ranked among Asia's 50 Best Bars.
  • BACKUP BAR MARTHA: Tokyo’s premier high-fidelity vinyl audio sanctuary just a few blocks away.

DAY 3: COASTAL EXCURSION

Fri, Oct 16
🎯 Day Preparation Notes (Early Departure) IMPORTANT: Early train departure at 07:30 AM! Pick up fresh pastries from Crossroad Bakery Ebisu or egg salads (tamago sando) + canned coffee from Lawson/7-Eleven the evening before to eat quickly in your hotel room before leaving.
🚆 Transit to Kamakura (~50 mins): Take the direct JR Shonan-Shinjuku Line train from Ebisu Station straight to Kamakura Station.

08:15 Bamboo Forest & Matcha @ Hokokuji Temple

Enter past the moss garden towards the main Zen hall. Sip freshly whisked ceremonial matcha at the Kyokutai-an Tea House inside 2,000 towering bamboo stalks.

🚶 Kamakura Walk (15 mins): Walk past Tsurugaoka Hachimangu Shrine, then stroll down Komachi-dori pedestrian street. Stop at Kamakura Beniya to buy their legendary Kurumicco pastries.

13:30 Great Buddha (Daibutsu) @ Kotoku-in

The iconic 13.35-meter open-air bronze Great Buddha statue, cast in 1252.

14:30 Coastal Views @ Hase-dera Temple

View the gilded 11-headed wooden Kannon statue, then head to the Observation Terrace to enjoy sweeping views across Sagami Bay while having green tea ice cream.

🚃 Retro Enoden Streetcar (20 mins): Ride the retro green Enoden electric streetcar along the Pacific shoreline, passing the famous ocean crossing at Kamakura-kokomae.

16:30 Enoshima Island & Sea Candle Sunset 🌇

Walk across the pedestrian bridge to Enoshima Island. Walk up shrine steps to the Enoshima Sea Candle observation tower for sunset views over Sagami Bay (and Mt. Fuji).

🍽️ Day 3 Meal & Beverage Plan

  • Lunch (12:00): Matsubara-an. Incredible handmade soba institution housed inside a restored early-20th-century Japanese coastal villa. Order fresh soba with crispy duck tempura.
  • BACKUP LUNCH Kamakura Cinema Soba: Historic casual buckwheat soba shop near Kamakura station.
  • Dinner (18:30): Tobiccho (Enoshima). Eat fresh or boiled baby sardine rice bowls (Shirasu-don).
  • Nightcap (20:30): Train back to Ebisu; vinyl nightcap at A10 Bar (hidden behind fake coin lockers).

DAY 4: VINTAGE, CATS & SHINJUKU

Sat, Oct 17
🎯 Day Preparation Notes A perfect geographic progression! Move smoothly from morning shrines to vintage shopping, a hidden cat temple, a retro neighborhood tram, and sunset in Shinjuku.

08:00 Shinto Morning Ritual @ Meiji Jingu

Arrive early before weekend crowds. Bow at the Harajuku Torii Gate, then observe the 08:00 AM morning Shinto ritual (Nikku-sai) conducted by priests in the main courtyard.

🚶 Omotesando Walk: Grab coffee, then take an architectural walk down Omotesando Boulevard past Prada Aoyama and SunnyHills.
🚇 Transit to Shimokitazawa (~8 mins): Take the Chiyoda/Odakyu line directly from Omotesando to Shimokitazawa Station.

13:00 Vinyl & Vintage @ Shimokitazawa

Tokyo’s bohemian heart. Spend two hours digging through used record stores (Flash Disc Ranch or Disk Union) and endless vintage clothing racks.

🚇 Transit to Gotokuji (~6 mins): Hop back on the Odakyu Line going west just a few stops to Gotokuji Station.

15:15 The Cat Temple @ Gotokuji

Walk 10 mins from the station to this quiet temple, the birthplace of the maneki-neko (beckoning cat). It is packed with thousands of white cat statues left for good luck.

🚃 Setagaya Retro Tram Ride (~12 mins): Walk 5 minutes to Miyanosaka Station. Board the Setagaya Line (Tokyo's adorable 2-car retro tram) and ride it through the neighborhood backyards up to Shimo-Takaido. Then transfer to the Keio Line into Shinjuku Station!

16:30 Golden Hour Sunset 🌇 @ Metro Building

Ride the high-speed elevator to the 45th-floor South Observatory of the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building for free golden hour views across Tokyo's expanse.

17:45 Yakitori & Alleyways @ Omoide Yokocho

Preserved 1940s post-war alleyway network ("Memory Lane") packed with smoky counter-only yakitori stalls. Grab a charcoal-grilled chicken skewer and a draft beer.

🍽️ Day 4 Meal & Beverage Plan

  • Coffee (09:15): The Roastery by Nozy Coffee. Single-origin espresso bar on Cat Street.
  • Lunch (11:30): Maisen Aoyama. Famous tonkatsu housed inside a renovated Showa-era public bathhouse.
  • BACKUP LUNCH Gyukatsu Motomura Harajuku: Rare beef cutlets flash-fried and seared on individual stone grills.
  • Dinner (19:30): Ramen Nagi Golden Gai. Up a steep staircase in Golden Gai. Famous for intense broth made from 20 varieties of dried sardine (niboshi).
  • BACKUP DINNER Tatsunoya Shinjuku: Legendary pork-bone tonkotsu ramen with charred tsukemen dipping broth.
  • Late Night Bar (21:00): Craft lemon sours surrounded by rare books at Open Book inside Golden Gai.

DAY 15: EAST TOKYO RETURN

Wed, Oct 28
🎯 Day Preparation Notes Welcome back to Tokyo from your regional trip! Check into your East Tokyo (Asakusa/Kuramae) hotel, settle in, and get ready for a relaxed shitamachi evening along the Sumida River.

15:30 SANAA Architecture @ Sumida Hokusai Museum

Striking angular mirrored-aluminum building designed by Pritzker Prize winner Kazuyo Sejima (SANAA). Houses the works of famous woodblock artist Katsushika Hokusai, creator of The Great Wave off Kanagawa.

🚶 Walk to Sumida River (15 mins): Walk through East Tokyo's quiet neighborhoods towards the water.

17:00 Sumida Riverwalk Sunset Spot 🌇

Stroll along the elevated Sumida Riverwalk footbridge as the sun sets behind Tokyo Skytree and the riverside skyline.

18:00 Historic "Electric Brandy" @ Kamiya Bar (1880)

Japan's first Western-style bar, operating out of an official 1921 Taisho-era building. Order a standing glass of Denki Bran ("Electric Brandy"), a Meiji-era herbal spirit blend.

🍽️ Day 15 Meal Plan

  • Dinner (19:30): Hoppy Street. A 1950s post-war drinking street lined with plastic-curtain izakayas. Famous for slow-simmered beef tendon stew (nikomi) and low-alcohol Hoppy mixed with shochu.
  • BACKUP 1 Yakiniku Beast: High-grade Wagyu beef yakiniku izakaya.
  • BACKUP 2 Asakusa Ichimon: Historic Edo-style tuna hotpot (negi-ma nabe) inside a traditional merchant house.

DAY 16: ANCIENT SENSŌ-JI & CHEF KNIVES

Thu, Oct 29
🎯 Day Preparation Notes Today is a beautifully smooth, zero-transit walking day. Walk right out of your hotel to see Sensō-ji at its most silent and magical hour before the city wakes up.

07:30 Silent Morning @ Sensō-ji Temple

Tokyo's oldest temple (founded in 645 AD). Pass under the 700kg red paper lantern at Kaminarimon in perfect morning silence. Walk down the 250-meter stone approach line before shops open, bathe your hands in sacred smoke at the Jokoro burner, and draw an Omikuji fortune scroll.

🚶 Walk to Kappabashi (10 mins): Stroll through Asakusa’s old residential lanes heading west just as the kitchenware shops begin to unlock their doors for the day.

10:00 Hand-Forged Knives @ Kappabashi

The premier street in Tokyo for buying hand-forged Japanese chef knives. Visit Kama-Asa and Tsubaya for high-end blades with complimentary custom name-engraving. For ceramics, visit Dengama and Tsuchi-ya for glassware.

🚶 Kuramae Stroll (12 mins): Walk south along the river towards Kuramae’s renovated industrial warehouse district ("Tokyo's Brooklyn").

14:00 Custom Stationery @ Kakimori (Kuramae)

Order custom hand-bound notebooks and bespoke bottled fountain pen ink at Kakimori.

🍽️ Day 16 Meal & Beverage Plan

  • Breakfast (08:45): February Cafe. Famous for thick-cut, buttered Shokupan (Japanese milk bread) toast and excellent pour-over coffee.
  • Lunch (12:30): Namiki Yabusoba. Founded in 1913, this historic institution serves handmade buckwheat noodles with a concentrated, dark dipping broth.
  • BACKUP LUNCH 1 Dote no Iseya: Historic tempura in a wooden building that survived the 1923 earthquake.
  • BACKUP LUNCH 2 Unatoto: Casual charcoal-grilled eel over rice.
  • Afternoon Sweets: Enjoy single-origin bean-to-bar hot chocolate inside a renovated factory at Dandelion Chocolate.
  • Specialty Coffee (15:30): World-class light-roast pour-over coffee at Leaves Coffee Roasters or organic green tea at Nakamura Tea Life Store.
  • Dinner (18:30): Craft beer and artisan burgers at McLean Old Burger Stand or river views at Cielo y Rio.

DAY 17: TSUKIJI, GINZA & YANESEN

Fri, Oct 30
🎯 Day Preparation Notes Fish market morning! Head to Tsukiji Outer Market at 08:00 AM on an empty stomach for a street-food breakfast crawl. Friday is the perfect day as you beat the intense weekend crowds!
🚇 Metro Transit to Tsukiji (~15 mins): Take the Toei Asakusa Line direct to Higashi-ginza/Tsukijishijo Station.

08:00 Street Food Crawl @ Tsukiji Outer Market

Tokyo's iconic historic fish market. Eat sweet rolled dashi omelets at Yamacho, slow-simmered horumon-don beef stew at Kitsuneya, and fresh Uni (sea urchin) / grilled scallops from seafood stalls.

🚶 Walk to Ginza (10 mins): Leave the bustling fish market and cross over into Ginza, Tokyo's premier luxury and architectural district.

10:00 Architecture & Depachika @ Ginza

Stroll down Chuo-dori. Admire the interior art at Ginza Six. Head down into the basement food hall (depachika) of the historic Mitsukoshi department store to marvel at perfect $100 melons and wagashi.

🚇 Transit to Yanesen (~25 mins): Take the Tokyo Metro from Ginza Station up to Nezu Station.

12:30 Contemporary Art @ SCAI The Bathhouse

A premier contemporary art gallery housed inside a 200-year-old renovated public bathhouse (sento) with preserved shoe lockers and high wooden ceilings. Visit nearby Asakura Museum of Sculpture for courtyards and tatami tea rooms.

15:30 Sunset Spot 🌇 @ Yuyake Dandan & Yanaka Ginza

Explore the Showa-era shopping street and watch the golden hour lighting down the sunset steps (Yuyake Dandan) around 17:00.

🍽️ Day 17 Meal & Beverage Plan

  • Market Coffee: Turret Coffee (Tsukiji). High-grade espresso served from a shop built around a retro fish market turret truck.
  • Morning Coffee (11:30): Walk through the peaceful stone paths of Yanaka Cemetery to grab a coffee at Kayaba Coffee (a restored 1938 wooden Kissaten).
  • Lunch (13:30): Kamachiku (Nezu). Handmade thick-cut udon served inside a 100-year-old converted brick warehouse overlooking a serene stone garden.
  • BACKUP LUNCH Soba Shinkawa: Traditional handmade buckwheat soba in Nezu.
  • Afternoon Snack: Famous minced beef cutlet (Menchi Katsu) on Yanaka Ginza street. Enjoy local craft beer in a restored 1938 wooden house at Yanaka Beer Hall.
  • Dinner (18:30): Post-war under-the-tracks izakaya market walk at Ameyoko Market in Ueno; craft cocktails at Cocktail Works Ueno.

DAY 18: EDO GARDENS & BOOKTOWN

Sat, Oct 31
🎯 Day Preparation Notes Explore Jimbocho (Japan's booktown), 17th-century landscape gardens, and Kagurazaka's historic geisha stone alleys.

08:30 Single-Origin Pour-Over @ Glitch Coffee

Legendary light-roast specialty roaster offering flight tastings of rare beans. Stroll past dozens of vintage second-hand bookstores and antique print shops in Jimbocho.

10:30 Edo Landscape @ Koishikawa Kōrakuen Garden

Tokyo's oldest surviving feudal-era landscape garden, commissioned in 1629. Features miniature Chinese bridges, maple trees, and reflection ponds.

16:00 Kengo Kuma Shrine & Kagurazaka Alleys

Visit Akagi Shrine, rebuilt in 2010 by master architect Kengo Kuma using glass and natural wood. Explore Kakurenbo Yokocho, stone-paved alleys where geisha houses once operated.

🍽️ Day 18 Meal & Beverage Plan

  • Lunch (13:00): Scallop tsukemen dipping noodles at Hotate Biyori or rich broth at Tsujita Akihabara.
  • Afternoon Coffee (17:30): Syphon coffee inside 1955 subterranean Showa kissaten Sabouru.
  • Dinner (19:00): Hidden izakaya dinner at Kagurazaka Ishibotoke or French-Japanese fusion at Lugdunum Bouchon Lyonnais.
