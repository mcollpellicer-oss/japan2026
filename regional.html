Regional Leg: Kanazawa & Yamanaka

Oct 18 – Oct 23 | Crafts, Gardens & Onsen Sanctuary

📍 Base 1: HOTEL AMANEK Kanazawa (Oct 18-20 & 22-23)
Focus: Minimalist wood design, central Katamachi location, Edo-period gardens, geisha districts, samurai lanes, and Sea of Japan seafood.

♨️ Base 2: Shirasagiyu Tawaraya (Oct 20-22)
Focus: 800-year-old ryokan, Kakusenkei Gorge river views, open-air rotenburo bathing, and multi-course kaiseki dining.

SHINKANSEN & GEISHA QUARTERS

Sun, Oct 18
🎯 Day Preparation Notes Travel day! Check out of your Ebisu hotel and grab a relaxed coffee. Buy a regional *ekiben* (bento box) at Tokyo Station before boarding the express Hokuriku Shinkansen.

10:22 Hokuriku Shinkansen @ Tokyo Station

💡 Train Logistics & Ekiben

Board the Kagayaki 509 (All-Seats Reserved) departing at 10:22 AM.

  1. STEP 1 Arrive at Tokyo Station by 09:40 AM.
  2. STEP 2 Visit the massive "Ekibenya Matsuri" shop on the central concourse to buy your lunch boxes.
  3. STEP 3 Store large bags in the reserved oversized baggage area behind the last row of seats.
🚆 Kagayaki 509 to Kanazawa (2.5 hours): Eat your bento as you cross through the Japanese Alps. Arrives at Kanazawa Station at exactly 12:49 PM.

13:30 Check-in @ HOTEL AMANEK Kanazawa

Take a 10-minute taxi from Kanazawa Station to the hotel in Katamachi. Drop your bags, freshen up, and head back out to explore the historic eastern side of the city.

15:30 Wooden Architecture @ Higashi Chaya

💡 Exploration & Shima Teahouse

The largest preserved geisha district in Kanazawa. Because you are arriving in the late afternoon, the daytime tour bus crowds will be thinning out.

  1. STEP 1 Shima Teahouse: Pay the small entry fee to explore this flawless 1820 geisha house, a designated National Important Cultural Property.
  2. STEP 2 Gold Leaf: Kanazawa produces 99% of Japan's gold leaf. Pick up some artisan crafts or try gold-leaf soft serve ice cream.

17:30 Twilight Walk @ Kazue-machi Chaya

Walk across the Asano River just as the sun sets. This smaller, intimate geisha district runs right along the water. It is lit by traditional lanterns in the evening and is deeply cinematic.

19:00 Charcoal Izakaya Dinner @ Fuwari

Housed in a restored, atmospheric wooden townhouse (machiya) with an open kitchen. Famous for charcoal-grilled local seafood and exceptional hospitality. (Reservations highly recommended).

SUNRISE GARDENS & SAMURAI LANES

Mon, Oct 19
🎯 Day Preparation Notes Wake up early! Arriving at Kenroku-en at 7:00 AM guarantees you experience one of Japan's finest landscapes in total peace before the crowds arrive.

07:00 Morning Serenity @ Kenroku-en Garden

One of the "Three Great Gardens of Japan." Wander past the Kotojitoro stone lantern, the reflection ponds, and the oldest fountain in Japan in pristine morning silence.

08:30 Tea Ceremony @ Gyokusen'inmaru Garden

Located right next to Kanazawa Castle. Enter the modern Gyokusen-an rest house, sit on the tatami, and drink traditional whisked matcha while looking out over the terraced stone garden.

10:30 SANAA Architecture @ 21st Century Museum

Walk through the striking circular glass structure. Be sure to visit Leandro Erlich’s famous "Swimming Pool" installation.

🚶 Walk to Omicho Market (15 mins): Walk north through the city center towards the famous food market for a massive seafood lunch.

12:30 Sea of Japan Sushi @ Omicho Market

💡 Food Hall Notes

Kanazawa's 300-year-old kitchen. The seafood pulled from the Sea of Japan here is legendary.

  • The Order: Get in line for a *kaisendon* (a massive bowl of rice topped with thick cuts of fresh yellowtail, sweet shrimp, snow crab, and edible gold leaf).

15:00 Walk in Samurai Footsteps (Nagamachi)

💡 7-Stop Self-Guided Route Notes

Walk along the Ōnoshō Canal, admiring the earthen walls where samurai once lived.

  1. STOP 1 Nagamachi Yuzen-kan: Kaga silk dyeing center.
  2. STOP 2 Takada Family House: Restored gatekeepers' quarters.
  3. STOP 3 Ashigaru Museum: Lower-class samurai dwellings.
  4. STOP 4 Nomura-ke Residence: Wealthy samurai home with immaculate tatami rooms and famous inner gardens.
  5. STOP 5 Kanazawa Shinise & Maeda Museums: Samurai armor and historical displays.
  6. STOP 6 Kaburaki: Traditional Kutani pottery shop.
  7. STOP 7 Yanagiya Saraku: Soba dishes and house-brewed beer with garden views.

19:00 Legendary Izakaya Dinner @ Itaru Honten

Located conveniently near your hotel. This high-energy, classic izakaya is famous for its thick cuts of sashimi and specifically *Nodoguro* (blackthroat seaperch), Kanazawa's incredibly fatty and prized local fish. (Go early to queue if you don't have a reservation).

INTO THE GORGE (YAMANAKA ONSEN)

Tue, Oct 20
🎯 Day Preparation Notes Ryokan day! Leave your large suitcases at Hotel Amanek's front desk. Pack only a small overnight bag with your essentials for the next two nights to travel weightlessly into the mountains.
🚆 Train to Kaga Onsen Station (~20 mins): Take the limited express Thunderbird or Shirasagi train from Kanazawa Station directly to Kaga Onsen Station.

13:30 Check-in @ Shirasagiyu Tawaraya

💡 Arrival & Ryokan Etiquette

Take the hotel shuttle (or a quick taxi) from the station deep into Yamanaka Onsen.

  • The Vibe: Founded 800 years ago, this ryokan sits directly on the Kakusenkei Gorge.
  • The Protocol: Take off your shoes at the entrance, put on your provided yukata (cotton robe), and leave your worldly stress at the door.

15:00 Rotenburo Soak & River Views

Spend the afternoon soaking in the open-air baths (rotenburo). The natural hot spring water flows continuously while you look directly out over the rushing river and autumn trees.

18:30 Kaiseki Dinner (At Ryokan)

Experience traditional *kaiseki-ryori*—a multi-course dinner of hyper-seasonal, beautifully plated local ingredients, usually featuring local Kaga vegetables and fresh seafood.

THE ZERO-TRANSIT GORGE RETREAT

Wed, Oct 21
🎯 Day Preparation Notes You have absolutely no transit today. This is your pure "middle day" reward. While the one-night guests rush to check out, you get the baths and the village entirely to yourself.

08:30 Traditional Ryokan Breakfast

A massive, savory spread featuring grilled fish, tamagoyaki (rolled omelet), miso soup, pickled vegetables, and rice.

10:30 Mossy Paths @ Kakusenkei Gorge

Walk the 1.3-kilometer riverside trail winding through the gorge. The air is incredibly fresh, surrounded by moss, rock formations, and dense forest.

11:30 S-Curve Architecture @ Ayatori Bridge

Cross this strikingly modern, purple S-shaped steel bridge designed by Hiroshi Teshigahara, contrasting beautifully against the ancient forest. If the riverbed kawadoko (tea pavilions) are open below, stop for a matcha and sweet.

13:00 Casual Lunch & Craft Shopping @ Yuge-dori

💡 Town Stroll

Walk down the main village street.

  • Shopping: Yamanaka is famous for wood-turning lacquerware. Browse the small artisan studios for stunning wooden bowls.
  • Lunch: Grab a simple, hearty bowl of hot soba or freshly fried croquettes from a local shop front.

15:00 Afternoon Soak & Ryokan Relax

Return to Tawaraya, put your yukata back on, and take advantage of the empty baths before the new afternoon guests arrive. Followed by your second kaiseki dinner at 18:30.

ZEN RETURN TO KANAZAWA

Thu, Oct 22
🎯 Day Preparation Notes A slow, unhurried transition back to the city. No highway buses or rushed day trips—just a peaceful return to your hotel and a relaxed afternoon of modern Zen art.

10:00 Check-out & Return Transit

Take the shuttle back to Kaga Onsen Station, board the limited express train, and arrive back in Kanazawa before Noon.

12:30 Check-in @ HOTEL AMANEK Kanazawa

Check back into Amanek, reunite with your large suitcases held at the front desk, and drop your bags in your new room.

14:00 Minimalist Philosophy @ D.T. Suzuki Museum

Walk over to this stunning architectural space dedicated to the philosopher who brought Zen Buddhism to the West. Spend time sitting quietly at the "Water Mirror Bowl," a massive, dark reflection pool designed for pure contemplation.

16:00 Kutani Pottery & Nagamachi Stroll

💡 Afternoon Pottery Stroll Notes

Return to the earthen walls of the samurai district. Browse the artisan shops (like Kaburaki at Stop 6) for traditional Kutani pottery—highly detailed, vibrantly painted porcelain unique to Ishikawa Prefecture.

19:00 Casual Final Dinner @ Oriental Brewing

After multi-course kaiseki meals and high-end izakayas, enjoy a relaxed final night with local Kanazawa craft beer and wood-fired pizzas or casual pub food before heading to Kyoto the next morning!
