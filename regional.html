KANAZAWA DAY 1

Wednesday, Oct 21
🎯 DAY 1 TOP TIPS & STRATEGY
  • Bullet Train Arrival: Ride the Hokuriku Shinkansen straight from Tokyo Station to Kanazawa Station (~2.5h). Inspect the monumental wooden Tsuzumi-mon Gate at the station exit.
  • Kenroku-en Lighting: Visit Kenroku-en in the late afternoon to see the early preparation of *Yukitsuri* (traditional rope cones erected to protect ancient pine branches from heavy snow).

11:00 AM Arrival & Kenroku-en Garden

Arrive in Kanazawa. Check in / drop luggage at hotel. Walk straight into Kenroku-en, universally celebrated as one of Japan's "Three Great Landscape Gardens."

💡 Cultural Context: Kenroku-en & Six Attributes

Kenroku-en: Translates to the "Garden of Six Attributes." According to Chinese landscape theory, a perfect garden must balance six opposing qualities: spaciousness vs. seclusion, artifice vs. antiquity, water features vs. panoramic views.

Kotojitoro Lantern: The famous two-legged stone lantern standing at the edge of Kasumigaike Pond, designed to resemble the bridge of a Japanese koto zither.

02:00 PM Kanazawa Castle Park & Gyokusen'inmaru

Walk across the bridge into Kanazawa Castle Park. Visit the pristine, restored wooden **Gyokusen'inmaru Garden** and enjoy whisked matcha inside the traditional tea house overlooking the stepped stone walls.

05:00 PM Higashi Chaya Geisha District Twilight Walk

Explore the preserved dark-wood lattice teahouses (*Kagashimi*) of **Higashi Chaya**. Visit **Hakuichi** to try Kanazawa's famous edible gold-leaf ice cream.

🍽️ Kanazawa Day 1 Meal Plan

  • Lunch: Fresh Kantenba seafood or Kanazawa-style curry at Champion's Curry.
  • Matcha Break: Whisked matcha and wagashi at Gyokusenan inside Gyokusen'inmaru Garden.
  • Dinner: Fresh Sea of Japan seafood & Noto beef at Kotobukiya or local izakaya Itaru Honten (famous for blackthroat seaperch / Nodoguro).

KANAZAWA DAY 2

Thursday, Oct 22
🎯 DAY 2 TOP TIPS & STRATEGY
  • Omicho Market Seafood: Visit Omicho Market right at 8:30 AM for peak fresh crab and sea urchin before the lunch rush.
  • 21st Century Museum: Book tickets online for Leandro Erlich's famous "Swimming Pool" installation.

08:30 AM Omicho Market Morning Feast

Walk through the bustling corridors of Omicho Market, Kanazawa's "Kitchen" since the Edo period. Watch fishmongers prepare local snow crab and sweet shrimp.

10:30 AM Nagamachi Samurai District & Nomura House

Stroll between the preserved mud walls and cobblestone lanes of the **Nagamachi Samurai District**. Enter **Nomura Samurai House** to view a genuine Edo-period samurai garden and armor collection.

💡 Cultural Context: Nomura Samurai House

Nomura House: Home to a high-ranking samurai family serving the Maeda Clan. Its courtyard garden features a crystal-clear stream fed by the historic Onosho Canal, ancient bayberry trees, and a waterfall framed by stone lanterns.

02:00 PM 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art

Explore the circular glass museum building designed by SANAA, featuring modern art installations integrated into open courtyards.

🍽️ Kanazawa Day 2 Meal Plan

  • Breakfast / Seafood: Kaisen-don bowl overflowing with fresh local sashimi at Omicho Shokudo or Iki-iki Tei.
  • Coffee Break: Drip coffee at Curio Espresso & Vintage Design.
  • Dinner: Modern Kanazawa Kappo cuisine at Aoi or charcoal-grilled skewers at Fugetsu.

YAMANAKA ONSEN SANCTUARY

Friday, Oct 23
🎯 ONSEN DAY TOP TIPS & STRATEGY
  • Ryokan Onsen Etiquette: Wash thoroughly at the wooden sit-down shower stalls before stepping into the communal thermal waters. No swimwear allowed; small hand towels stay out of the bathwater.
  • Ryokan Check-in: Arrive by 03:00 PM to maximize relaxation, enjoy your private in-room tea service, and reserve your private open-air bath (*Rotenburo*) time slot.

12:30 PM Transit to Yamanaka Onsen Valley

Board the local shuttle or train down to Kaga-Onsen Station and transit into the lush mountain valley of **Yamanaka Onsen**.

01:30 PM Kakusenkei Gorge & Ayatori Bridge Walk

Walk along the serene 1.3km **Kakusenkei Gorge** stone path following the roaring mountain river. Cross the magenta, S-curved **Ayatori Bridge** designed by Hiroshi Teshigahara and visit the rustic **Koroan Tea House** set right along the water rapids.

💡 Cultural Context: Yamanaka Onsen & Matsuo Basho

800-Year Thermal History: Discovered over 1,300 years ago by a Buddhist monk, Yamanaka is famous for hydrogen-rich, mineral bathwaters praised by legendary haiku poet Matsuo Basho as one of Japan's top three healing hot springs.

Yamanaka Lacquerware (*Shuraku*): The town is world-renowned for exquisite hand-turned wood lacquerware, where artisans carve raw timber into delicate tea caddies and soup bowls showcasing natural wood grain.

03:30 PM Ryokan Check-In: Shirasagiyu Tawaraya

Check into **Shirasagiyu Tawaraya** (operating continuously for 800 years). Change into traditional *Yukata* robes, soak in the open-air hot spring baths overlooking the forest gorge, and prepare for your multi-course Kaiseki banquet.

🍽️ Ryokan Feast & Onsen Dining

  • Tea Welcome: In-room whisked green tea and artisan sweet upon arrival at the ryokan.
  • Ryokan Dinner Banquet: Multi-course seasonal *Kaiseki* meal served in your room/private dining hall, featuring Noto beef, fresh Sea of Japan crab, seasonal mountain vegetables (*Sansai*), and local Ishikawa sake.
  • Next Morning Breakfast: Traditional Japanese ryokan breakfast with grilled local fish, miso soup cooked over clay stoves, and mountain rice.
