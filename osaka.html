Osaka Food Experience & Koyasan Leg

Oct 26 – Oct 28 | Street Food Heaven, Izakaya Alleys & Sacred Mountain

🏨 Osaka Base: Centara Life Namba Hotel Osaka (€62.2 / Oct 26–27)
Focus: Deluxe Twin, Kuromon Market, Namba street food, specialty roasters, and direct access to Nankai Namba Station.

🧘 Koyasan Base: Shukubo Temple Stay (Oct 27–28)

🎒 Luggage Strategy: Store main suitcases in Nankai Namba Station multi-day lockers on Oct 27 morning; carry backpacks only to Koyasan!

🐙 Osaka Street Food & "Tapa Route" Essentials

Osaka is famous for Kuidaore ("eat until you drop"). Treat these iconic Osakan specialties like a Japanese tapeo route!

1. Takoyaki (Octopus Balls) Molten batter balls filled with octopus, topped with sweet brown sauce, kewpie mayo & bonito flakes.
📍 Wanaka Honten / Takoyaki Umaiya / Kogaryu
2. Okonomiyaki (Savory Pancake) Griddled cabbage pancake with pork belly, seafood, egg & sweet savory glaze.
📍 Fukutaro / Yukari Tensan / Chibo
3. Kushikatsu & Doteyaki Crispy deep-fried meat/veggie skewers (no double-dipping!) + miso-simmered beef tendon.
📍 Tori no Su / Kushikatsu Daruma / Wakabaya
4. Kitsune Udon & Oshizushi Thick chewy udon in golden dashi with sweet fried tofu + traditional pressed box sushi.
📍 Imai Honten / Mimiu Honten / Yoshino Sushi
5. Specialty Coffee & Drinks World-class light roasters & riverfront craft beer bars.
📍 Lilo Coffee / Mel Coffee / Beer Belly Tenma / Moto Coffee

KYOTO TRANSITION & NAMBA TAPA CRAWL

Mon, Oct 26
🎯 Day Preparation Notes Arrive from Kyoto, drop bags at Centara Life Namba, and jump straight into an epic food, market, and neon night crawl across Namba, Shinsekai, and Dotonbori!
🚆 Transit from Kyoto to Osaka (~45 mins): Keihan Line from Sanjo/Gion-Shijo straight to Yodoyabashi, or JR Special Rapid from Kyoto Station to Osaka/Namba (~¥570).

13:30 Check-in @ Centara Life Namba Hotel Osaka

Check into your Deluxe Twin room (€62.2). Settle in and unpack—you are perfectly situated in Namba near all top markets and food alleys.

15:00 Specialty Coffee Stop @ Lilo Coffee Roasters

Grab a single-origin pour-over at Lilo Coffee Roasters or Mel Coffee Roasters in Amerikamura.

16:30 Tsutenkaku Tower & Shinsekai Alleys 🌇

Head down to Shinsekai as dusk falls and the retro arcade lanterns light up around Tsutenkaku Tower. Start your "tapa route" with hot skewers at Tori no Su or Kushikatsu Daruma along with slow-simmered beef tendon (doteyaki).

19:00 Dotonbori Street Food Crawl & Glico Sign

Stroll through the neon-lit Dotonbori canal. Eat piping-hot molten takoyaki at Wanaka Honten (or Takoyaki Umaiya) and freshly grilled cabbage okonomiyaki at Fukutaro or Yukari.

🍽️ Day 1 Tapa Lineup & Nightcaps

  • Takoyaki Stop: Wanaka Honten or Takoyaki Umaiya.
  • Okonomiyaki Stop: Fukutaro or Yukari Tensan.
  • Kushikatsu & Doteyaki: Tori no Su (Shinsekai) or Wakabaya (Fukushima).
  • Late-Night Drinks: Local craft beers at Beer Belly Tenma or drinks along the Hozenji Yokocho stone alley.

ASCENT TO SACRED KOYASAN

Tue, Oct 27
🎯 Day Preparation Notes (Locker Strategy) CRITICAL LOGISTICS: Pack 1 small overnight backpack with 1 night of clothes and toilet items. Store your main suitcases in Nankai Namba Station multi-day coin lockers before boarding the train to Koyasan!
💡 Step-by-Step Koyasan Transit & Locker Routine
  1. STEP 1 08:30 AM: Check out of Centara Life Namba Hotel. Walk 5 mins to Nankai Namba Station.
  2. STEP 2 Store main suitcases in large multi-day coin lockers (3rd Floor North Gate / Central Gate, ~¥800/day).
  3. STEP 3 Purchase the Koyasan World Heritage Ticket (covers round-trip Nankai train, cable car, and unlimited Koyasan buses).
  4. STEP 4 Board Nankai Koya Line Express train to Gokurakubashi Station (~90 mins), then switch to the cable car up the mountain face (5 mins).

12:30 Arrival & Check-in @ Shukubo Temple Stay

Arrive in Koyasan, the sacred 1,200-year-old headquarters of Shingon Buddhism founded by monk Kobo Daishi. Check into your temple stay lodge (Shukubo).

14:00 Danjo Garan & Kongobu-ji Temple

Explore Danjo Garan, the central temple complex with its towering vermilion Konpon Daito pagoda. Visit Kongobu-ji, head temple of Shingon Buddhism, featuring Banryutei (Japan's largest rock garden depicting two dragons emerging from clouds).

17:30 Shojin Ryori Monastic Dinner

Dine on traditional Shojin Ryori—exquisite Buddhist vegetarian cuisine cooked without meat, fish, garlic, or onions, focusing on seasonal wild mountain plants, sesame tofu (*Goma-dofu*), and tempura.

19:30 Night Walk @ Okunoin Cemetery 🌙

Take an ethereal evening walk along the 2-kilometer stone path under ancient cedar trees lit by thousands of tall stone lanterns, leading to Kobo Daishi's mausoleum.

MARKET LUNCH, KITSUNE UDON & TOKYO RETURN

Wed, Oct 28
🎯 Day Preparation Notes Wake up early for temple prayers and the Goma fire ceremony before heading back down to Namba to retrieve your main bags and enjoy an epic market & noodle lunch before boarding the Shinkansen!

06:00 Morning Chant & Goma Fire Ritual

Participate in the monk's morning chanting ceremony (*Otsutome*) followed by the sacred *Goma* fire ritual, where wooden prayer sticks are burned in consecrated flames to purify desires.

🚃 Mountain Descent & Suitcase Retrieval (~2 hrs): Take the bus to Koyasan station, cable car down to Gokurakubashi, and Nankai Express train back to Nankai Namba Station. Retrieve your main suitcases directly from your station lockers!

11:30 Kuromon Market Crawl & Kitsune Udon Lunch

Walk 5 minutes from Namba Station through Kuromon Ichiba Market for sea urchin, fresh scallops, and oshizushi (pressed sushi at Yoshino Sushi). Sit down at Imai Honten or Mimiu Honten for authentic Osakan Kitsune Udon.

13:30 Shinkansen Transit @ Shin-Osaka Station

Take a 15-minute subway/taxi ride to Shin-Osaka Station. Board the bullet train (Shinkansen) bound for Tokyo Station (~2.5 hours) to begin your 4-night finale at Skytree View Kinshicho!

🍽️ Day 3 Lunch Lineup

  • Market Eats: Fresh sea urchin, grilled scallops & press sushi (Yoshino Sushi) at Kuromon Market.
  • Noodles: Kitsune Udon at Imai Honten or Mimiu Honten.
  • Coffee & Sweets: Pour-over at Moto Coffee or Brooklyn Roasting Company along the river.
